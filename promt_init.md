# Few-Shot Prompting

## Overview
Few-shot prompting involves providing the model with a few examples of the task within the prompt to guide its behavior.

## How to Use
- Include 2-5示例 (examples) that illustrate the desired input-output pattern.
- Keep examples concise and representative.
- Place examples before the actual query.
